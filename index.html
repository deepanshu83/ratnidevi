About Ratni Devi

Dedicated to protecting your vision

Established in 2014, Ratni Devi Eye Hospital provides eye care for families in Chirawa and across Jhunjhunu district. From routine check-ups and vision correction to surgical treatment, patients can access essential eye services at one local facility.

Modern Cataract Care

Advanced, injection-free laser cataract surgery using ALCON PHACO technology.

Convenient On-Site Services

An in-house pharmacy and optical shop help patients access medicines and eyewear in one place.

Accessible for Visitors

Wheelchair-accessible entrance and parking make visits easier for patients and caregivers.
